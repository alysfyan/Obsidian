In-Text Citation  
1. Single Author

• Front sentence: Lim (2024) argues that…

• End sentence: …as argued by Lim (2024).

• End sentence (Parenthetical): ….did not agree with the idea (Lim, 2024)

2. Two Authors

• Front sentence: Davidson and Lim (2022) explain that…

• End sentence: …as explained by Davidson and Lim (2022).

• End sentence (Parenthetical): Energy use, maintenance replacement and durability also inform investment decisions (Davidson & Lim, 2022).

3. More than three authors

• Front sentence: Lee et al. (2024) argue that…

• End sentence: …as argued by Lee et al. (2024).

• End sentence (Parenthetical): The local economy is shifting, and consumer spending patterns are highly unpredictable (Lee et al., 2024).

Extra points if you combine two sources together.  References must be in alphabetical order (If you did not arrange them alphabetically, just draw an arrow as to where it should be).
